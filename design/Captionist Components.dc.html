RULES OF USE
One primary action per screen; everything else is secondary, ghost or destructive. Any action without a visible result must confirm with a snackbar. Only one reaction or attachment surface may be open at a time. Reaction affordances are always the smiley-plus icon and always open a searchable toolbar. The uploader is one component — identical tabs, dropzone and file-ready card in both modes. Anonymity holds until the reveal: no names on vote cards in either mode. Mode-specific copy lives in the logic class, never in duplicated markup.
